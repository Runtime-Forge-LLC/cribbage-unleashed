Cribbage Unleashed Privacy Policy

Runtime Forge LLC · Effective October 1, 2026

This policy explains what information the Cribbage Unleashed game ("the app") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is made by Runtime Forge LLC ("we", "us"). If you have questions, contact us at runtimeforgellc@gmail.com.

Information we collect

InformationWhere it comes fromWhy we use it
Account ID, name and email addressWhen you sign in with Google (or Apple, on iOS), through Google Firebase AuthenticationTo create and secure your account and keep your progress tied to it
Display name and avatarWhat you choose in the appTo show your profile, and to show you to the other player in an online game
Game data: stats, progress, unlocked items, equipped cosmetics, and your Crib Cash and Fancy Dollarydoos balancesYour play in the appTo save your game and restore it on your devices

The summaries hold no account ID, name or email. You can turn them off anytime in the app (Profile → Share anonymous stats).

Some settings, such as game speed and tutorial progress, are stored only on your device.

What we don't do

  • We do not show ads, and we do not use advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.
  • We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Who processes your data

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions) to run accounts and store game data, and Google Play or the Apple App Store to process purchases. These providers handle data on our behalf under their own privacy terms: Firebase privacy, Google privacy policy, Apple privacy policy. Data may be stored and processed in the United States. It is encrypted in transit.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and game data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, game data and balances within 30 days. We may keep purchase records longer where the law requires it (for example, for tax and accounting). Gameplay statistics aren't linked to your account, so they can't be found or removed with it; we keep them only as long as they help us balance the game.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data from inside the app (Profile → Delete account), or by emailing runtimeforgellc@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". Deleting your account permanently removes your progress, items and unspent currency.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, and to object to how it is used. To make a request, email runtimeforgellc@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
